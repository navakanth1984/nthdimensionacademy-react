Legal & Data Ethics

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

Nth Dimension Academy ("we", "our", or "the Academy") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our students, enterprise clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy delineates how we handle personal identifiable information (PII) collected across our domain (https://nthdimensionacademy.com) and associated learning portals.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide high-quality education and mentorship:

  • Direct Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and organization details submitted voluntarily when inquiring about training batches or corporate enablement.
  • Course Progress & Telemetry: Anonymized interaction telemetry within our Learning Atlases and quizzes, utilized exclusively to optimize learning retention and curriculum design.
  • Technical Logs: Standard HTTP request logs including IP addresses, browser user agents, and referral paths to ensure operational uptime and prevent DDoS attacks.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following educational purposes:

  • Administering certification bootcamps, delivering live lecture access, and distributing curriculum guides.
  • Responding promptly to mentorship inquiries and corporate workshop proposals.
  • Maintaining academic records of completed lab assessments and issued certificates of completion.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with third-party advertisers or lead-generation brokers under any circumstances.

4. Data Retention & Security Protocols

All student inquiries and database records are safeguarded using industry-standard TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Access to administrative systems is protected by role-based access control and multi-factor authentication. Inactive contact records are securely purged in accordance with standard data minimization principles.
